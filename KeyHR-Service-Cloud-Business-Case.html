The Opportunity

Where Key HR's service operation is leaving value on the table

Today's case intake, routing, and reporting processes weren't built for the volume Key HR now handles. Four gaps show up consistently in how the team works — each one maps directly to a Service Cloud / Agentforce capability.

⚙️

Case intake & routing

Manual routing slows down triage and makes it harder to lean on the offshore support team to control cost.

📈

Upsell & cross-sell visibility

Account and contact-level reporting isn't rich enough to systematically surface expansion opportunities inside service conversations.

⏱️

Time to resolution

Simple, low-complexity cases consume time that could be redirected toward key accounts and higher-value work.

📋

Survey & reporting visibility

Customer survey data is hard to report on and lacks the visibility leadership needs to track service quality and identify trends.
